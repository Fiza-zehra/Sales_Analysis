# Data Visualization Project: Bitesbyfizzy Sales Analysis 🍰📊

A data visualization project built for my own bakery brand **Bitesbyfizzy** using real sales data.  
This project turns 9 months of business data into actionable insights with Python.

## 📌 Project Overview

As a founder, I wanted to stop guessing and start making data-driven decisions for my bakery.  
I analyzed sales data from **Nov 2025 to Aug 2026** to understand what products sell, which flavours work, and where the demand is.

Dataset:  orders |  Products |  Flavours |  Cities

## 🎯 Key Insights

- Top Product: Glass Cake is the best-selling product
- Top Flavour: Chocolate Fudge drives the most orders
- Top City: Hyderabad generates the highest revenue.
- Trend: Clear monthly sales pattern identified

## 📊 Visualizations

The project includes 4 main charts:

1.  Top Selling Products - Bar chart showing quantity sold per product
2.  Revenue by City - Bar chart comparing city-wise revenue
3.  Monthly Sales Trend - Line chart showing growth from Nov 2025 to Aug 2026
4.  Bestselling Flavours- Horizontal bar chart of flavour popularity

All charts follow a consistent pink brand theme.
